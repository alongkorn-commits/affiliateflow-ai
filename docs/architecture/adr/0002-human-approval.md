# ADR-0002: Human approval is mandatory for public publishing

- Status: Accepted
- Context: AI-generated marketing content can contain inaccurate claims, policy violations or incorrect links.
- Decision: No agent or worker may publish publicly without a valid human approval for the exact immutable content version and destination account.
- Consequences: approval adds latency but reduces legal, brand and account risk. Approval must be invalidated by any content change.
