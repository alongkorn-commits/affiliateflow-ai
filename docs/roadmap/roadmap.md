# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Repository, local dependencies, Laravel, React, authentication, database migrations, CI.

## Phase 1 — Product & research
Affiliate URL intake, manual product facts, provenance, research agent, product review.

## Phase 2 — Campaign & copy
Campaign planning, strategist, copywriter, structured content drafts and platform adaptations.

## Phase 3 — Creative factory
Image provider, TTS, FFmpeg, media storage, asset provenance and deterministic QA.

## Phase 4 — Approval & export
Immutable versions, human approval, revision flow, export package, publishing kill switch.

## Phase 5 — Publishing
Connector interface, mock connector, then one live platform after API access and review. Expand to other channels individually.

## Phase 6 — Analytics
Platform metrics, affiliate report import, cost analytics, experiment recommendations.

## Suggested 8-week target
Week 1 foundation; week 2 products; week 3 strategy/copy; week 4 media; week 5 QA/approval; week 6 one connector or export; week 7 more connectors where approved; week 8 analytics and stabilization.

Timeline is a planning estimate, not a guarantee. API approvals and media-provider access may extend it.
